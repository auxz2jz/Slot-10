# Common construction standards

## Lumber
- Ground-contact pressure-treated lumber where exposed to weather/ground proximity.
- Nominal dimensions are not actual dimensions: 4×4 = 3.5×3.5 in; 6×6 = 5.5×5.5 in; 2×4 = 1.5×3.5 in; 2×6 = 1.5×5.5 in; 2×8 = 1.5×7.25 in.
- Reject severe checks, splits, decay, major edge wane, or large knots in safety-critical rack/pivot zones.

## Fasteners
- Safety-critical frame joints: through-bolts with locking nuts and large washers/backing plates.
- Exterior screws may hold decking, trim, pads and noncritical blocking, but are not substitutes for rack/pivot through-bolts.
- Hardware contacting pressure-treated lumber must be compatible with preservative chemicals (hot-dip galvanized or appropriate stainless).

## Steel / moving parts
- Use steel pipe/shaft at bar rests, safeties, pivots, loading horns and bodyweight handles.
- Fit bushings/bearings at moving pivots; do not let steel shafts grind directly in wood.
- Deburr every steel edge and cap exposed tube ends.
- Outdoor removable cable/padded components should be stored in the deck box when practical.

## Universal receiver
- Planning envelope: 2.5-in square steel receiver tube accepting a 2-in attachment shank, retained by a rated 5/8-in pin.
- Receiver base bolts through structural framing/steel plate, not merely the 3/4-in deck skin.
- Final receiver steel wall thickness and weld details should be selected/reviewed for the intended load.

## Fit-up rule
The exact purchased bench and pulley hardware must be physically measured before drilling final indexing or accessory-clearance holes. The CAD uses reasonable envelopes but does not claim manufacturer-specific dimensions.
