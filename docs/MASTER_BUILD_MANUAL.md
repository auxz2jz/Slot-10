## Safety boundary
These plans are a detailed fabrication starting point for a DIY outdoor gym, not stamped structural/mechanical engineering. Highest-risk components include rack/safeties, leg-press pivot/stops, bodyweight mounts, cable anchors, and site anchorage. Verify site-specific footings, lumber condition, steel sizes, welds, and fastener grades before heavy loading. Test unloaded first and progressively load while standing clear.
